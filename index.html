Non OneJump Achievement

Besides from the standard OneJump which only takes you 1 jump to win, there are several courses through out different servers for us to beat.

There are 2 types of map in general, segmented and rankup. Segmented map is basically level based where you can practice your strategy finding skills mostly without risking losing much progress; whereas rankup focuses on precision and accuracy, i.e. if you fail, you lose progress or even go back to start, making the map more challenging significantly yet adrenaline inducing.

Unfortunately, I am not a "rankup guy", I play Hypixel Housing which is basically segmented map for fun mostly. That doesn't mean all the maps in Housing is easy though, in fact, some of the maps are extremely challenging that it cost me days to finish. Here're some examples below.
